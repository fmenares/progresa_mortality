Standard errors are clustered at the municipality level. 1996 is a reference year and the regression models are not weighted. Source: Authors’ calculations using data on deaths and population from the Instituto Nacional de Estadistica y Geografia and Mexican Census data.}\added{Notes: This figure provides two alternative specifications complementing the main Figure~2 (weighted EMR, Seguro Popular controls). Panel (a) presents the unweighted event study for the Elder Mortality Rate (EMR), which is the specification closest to the approach of Barham and Rowberry (2013) and allows assessment of whether weighting alters the pattern. Panel (b) presents the weighted event study for the Age-Adjusted Mortality Rate (AAMR), which directly standardizes for population age structure and provides robustness to composition effects. Both panels display interaction coefficients between Progresa intensity in 1999 and year indicators for pooled, male, and female populations, with 1996 as the reference year. Both include Seguro Popular intensity as a control. The null finding is consistent across both panels, alternative specifications, and both sexes. The sample is restricted to highly marginalized municipalities ($\textit{gm\_mun\_1990} = 4$ or $5$). 95\% confidence intervals shown.}
}
\end{figure}

Row (E) replaces the two-year lag with a three-year lag (year-varying denominator, weighted). All regressions include municipality and year fixed effects.     \floatfoot{Notes: This table reports difference-in-differences estimates, using 1998 as baseline, of the effect of Progresa on household expenditure outcomes. Columns~(1)--(2) report log food expenditure and the food expenditure share; columns~(3)--(4) report log health expenditure and the health expenditure share. Because only eligible (poor) households receive the transfer, Panel~B's differential isolates the role of the direct food transfer (\textit{apoyo alimentario}) among households where the elderly member is surely the recipient.

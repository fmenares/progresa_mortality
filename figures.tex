\section{Pre-Trend Figures: Mechanisms Event Study}
\label{app:pretrend_figs}

Each panel plots pre-period interaction coefficients from
\texttt{reghdfe outcome ib1996.year\#c.inten[\textit{yr}]}
with year and municipality fixed effects, survey-expansion weights
(\texttt{exp\_factor}), and standard errors clustered by municipality.
Reference year: 1996 ($\hat\beta=0$ by construction).
Bars are 95\% confidence intervals.
Observations above the 99th percentile of each outcome--year cell are excluded.
Three series per panel: Pooled (navy, circle), Female (cranberry, square),
Male (forest green, triangle).
